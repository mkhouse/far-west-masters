Mammoth Masters Camp (Dec 1–4) and Mammoth GS Races (Dec 5–6)

Camp registration closes Sunday, November 29. Lodging reservations for camp and Mammoth GS races close Saturday, November 28.

Masters Camp, December 1–4

Mammoth Mountain hosts its Masters Camp Tuesday through Friday, December 1–4, 2026, 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. daily. All four days focus on GS training. The camp is open to the public for guests 18 and older, and new members must take part in a brief ski assessment to be eligible. The schedule is subject to change for wind and weather. A FIS-certified race helmet is required for GS.

• Sign your camp waiver: Masters Camp & Opener 2026 Waiver

• Register for the camp: Masters Camp 2026 Registration

• Book discounted lodging online: Masters Camp & Opener 2026 Lodging. Or call (800) MAMMOTH (800-626-6684) and reference the group “USSS/FW Masters & Opener 2026”, Group ID 38263. Resort fee $10.

All camp links, plus lift ticket details, are in our website article: Mammoth Masters Camp December 1–4 and Opening Races December 5–6.

2025-26 Season Party and Awards, Friday, December 4

Join us Friday, December 4, at 5:30 p.m. at Mammoth Brewing Company, 18 Lake Mary Road. We couldn’t hold our usual season finals and banquet last spring, so we’ll celebrate the 2025-26 season here. We’ll present trophies to the top 3 racers in each age class and Open class, plus the season leader bibs. If you’re in Mammoth that Friday, please join us.

Mammoth GS Races, December 5–6

The camp wraps up Friday, December 4, and racing starts the next day, so camp attendees can put the drills and gate training to work. Registration is open.

• $45 per race. Early registration deadline: midnight Sunday, November 29 ($5 per race late fee after that). Registration closes Thursday, December 3, at 7:00 p.m. No late entries or day-of-race signups.

• Full details and lift ticket information: December GS race flyer (PDF)

Register for the Dec 5–6 GS races
 

Mammoth Speed Weekend: Dave McCoy Memorial, January 22–24

Mammoth’s speed weekend adds a GS this year. The first SG each day counts toward the Masters National Speed Series (NSS).

• Lodging reservations are open. Call 800-MAMMOTH (800-626-6684) and reference “DAVE MCCOY MEMORIAL FW MASTERS,” Group ID 38267, or book online. Reservation cutoff: January 16, 2027. Resort fee $10. Reservations are subject to the deposit and cancellation policies shown when you book.

• Races: Fri 1/22 and Sat 1/23: two SGs each day (the first each day is NSS). Sun 1/24: GS.

• $55 per SG, $45 for the GS. Early registration deadline: midnight Sunday, January 17 ($5 per race late fee after that). Registration closes 7:00 p.m. Tuesday, January 19. No late entries or day-of-race signups.

• Full details: January Speed Series race flyer (PDF)

Want to be scored in Open class?

Open class is optional. Instead of your age class, you are scored against all ages for race awards and season scoring. Elect it once and it applies to all of your races this season, or choose a single event. Email dlewis@farwestmasters.org to elect it.

IMPORTANT: If you want to be scored in Open class, you MUST elect it by noon the day before the first race you want it for (before the close of the start list). Day-of-race Open class changes will not be accepted.

See who has signed up on the Open class competitors page, and read the scoring rules.
 

Text notifications

We send time-sensitive membership and race announcements by text message (SMS), in addition to email. Sign up with your name, email and mobile number. The form never asks for a password or payment; if it does, close it and email membership@farwestmasters.org.

Sign up for text notifications

Far West Masters Scholarship Awards Announced

A committee of eight reviewed this year’s scholarship applicants, and once again the candidates were impressive.

We are giving out $20,000 in regular scholarships and $10,000 in special projects funding for athletes who qualify for events such as NorAms, U.S. National Championships, and sometimes World Junior Championships. We also awarded $20,000 in college scholarships over the summer. See the full announcement.

Look for these athletes at our races. Cheer them on when they forerun, and stay for the awards ceremony to hear them talk about their goals and interests.

A thank-you from scholarship recipient Brody Mitchell

Brody wrote to the scholarship program in April, after his first year of FIS racing. He set out to lower his points into the 80s and finish most of his races, and he did both, even with an ankle injury halfway through the season.
