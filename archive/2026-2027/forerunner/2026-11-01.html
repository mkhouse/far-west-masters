A Note from the Chairman

Here we are on the 1st of November, we have a covering of snow on the mountains and the new season is only weeks away. As you know we start the season with a training camp in Mammoth from December 1st to 4th followed by the season opener, two GS races on Dec 5th and 6th. To help kick the season off we have decided to hold a party on Friday evening at the Mammoth Brewing Company at 18 Lake Mary Road. Seeing as we were unable to have our usual season finals and banquet at the end of last season due to the timing of the Nationals in Mammoth, we plan to use this pre-season party to celebrate the 2025-2026 season. We will present trophies for the top 3 racers in each age class, and open class. We will also present the season leader bibs.

If you are going to be in Mammoth on Friday December 4th please plan to join us for the event. We will start at 5:30 and look forward to seeing as many of you as possible for a fun evening.

Hamish
 

Mammoth Masters Camp: December 1–4

Mammoth Masters Race Camp is geared toward helping the competitive alpine ski racer prepare for the Alpine Masters Ski Racing season. The camp includes drills, gate training, video analysis, dynamic progressions, and more.

Mammoth Mountain is hosting the camp Tuesday through Friday, December 1–4, 2026, from 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. each day. All four days focus on GS training. The camp is open to guests 18 and older. New members must take part in a brief ski assessment to be eligible. The schedule is subject to adjustment based on wind and weather conditions.

Immediately following the camp is the Masters Opener, the annual kickoff for Far West Masters Racing, with two GS races at Mammoth on December 5–6.

Registration closes Sunday, November 29.

• Sign your camp waiver: Masters Camp & Opener 2026 Waiver

• Register for the camp: Masters Camp 2026 Registration

• Book discounted lodging online: Masters Camp & Opener 2026 Lodging. Or call (800) MAMMOTH and reference the group name “USSS/FW Masters & Opener 2026”, Group ID 38263.

• Lift tickets for yourself: Masters Camp Attendee Lift Tickets. Redeem your ticket in HQ.

• Lift tickets for family members: Masters Camp Family Lift Tickets. Redeem family tickets in HQ.

U.S. Ski & Snowboard and Far West Masters Membership

U.S. Ski & Snowboard sign-up can be confusing. Check out their Getting Started guide for help with membership types and the basics.

Most Masters should select the “Alpine Master” membership type with “No Club Affiliation.” Only choose “Alpine Master w/ requirements” if your club role requires SafeSport training and background checks.

You need both a U.S. Ski and Snowboard membership and a Far West Masters membership to participate in our races.

• Far West Masters membership on Masters AdminSkiRacing. Dues are $20 for the season through November 22, then $25.

Scholarship Program: USA Race Suits

The U.S. Ski Team donates race suits to the Far West Masters Scholarship Program. These are new suits that were never used by the team and retail for about $1,700. The program raises money by selling them for $500 to Masters racers and to athletes who are not in the scholarship program. Sponsored scholarship athletes pay $100, so they have “a little ski in the game.” In special situations, the program also helps an athlete who needs a suit but is not in the program.

Annabeth Au, a second-year U14 racer with Team Palisades Tahoe, is one of those athletes. She wrote to thank the program for her USA speedsuit: “I truly appreciate your support!” Read the full post.

This year’s scholarship recipients will be announced later in November.

Interested in a suit? Contact Eddie Mozen through the Scholarship Program page on the Far West Masters website.
