President’s Letter

As you may know, some of our Far West Masters athletes were among more than 30 Masters racers from around the world who were in Nevados de Chillan, Chile, for a Masters ski camp and the first event of the FIS Masters Alpine Skiing World Cup when a fire broke out in their hotel.

Thankfully all hotel guests and staff were evacuated safely and no serious injuries were reported. The hotel was destroyed and most racers lost all their equipment and belongings in the fire.

We can only imagine what a devastating experience this was and our thoughts are with everyone impacted by this tragedy.

US Ski and Snowboard and US Masters are actively looking for ways to help those impacted replace some of the gear they lost and be ready to race in time for the coming season.

On a happier note, thanks again to Mike Braun for putting together our schedule for the coming season.

As usual we start the season in December in Mammoth with a masters training camp followed by two days of GS racing.

In the new year we go back to Mammoth in January followed by a busy February with visits to Sierra at Tahoe, Northstar and Sugar Bowl.

March sees us back at Alpine and Palisades Tahoe. In total we have 17 races scheduled over 13 days: 6 SG, 5 GS and 6 SL, something for everyone to look forward to I think.

Following our last weekend of the season at Palisades Tahoe the Masters National Championships are being held in Alyeska, AK from March 21–27.

Other opportunities to race out of division this year include the FIS World Cup Finals in Big Sky, MT from April 7 to 10 and the Eastern Regionals at Gore Mountain from February 26 to 28.

For the speed fans among us there are two opportunities for DH races this year at Soldier Mountain, ID and Ski Cooper, CO, in addition to the DH at Nationals.

If you are able to travel a little further the 42nd annual Over The Hill Downhill at Silver Star Resort in Canada has a reputation for being a great DH event.

The end of season PNW races at Mt. Bachelor, OR on April 1 to 4 might be an opportunity for a final race weekend without having to travel too far.

We are in the early stages of planning but if possible we would like to hold an event in Mammoth on December 4th to celebrate all of our winners from last season. Hopefully more details to come on that in the next newsletter.

Please take a look at the information on our annual membership renewals, Far West Masters membership renewals open on Admin Ski Racing on October 1st. The deadline for US Ski and Snowboard membership renewals before the price increase is October 15th.

All the best,

Hamish
 

How to Register

To race this season you need two current memberships: U.S. Ski & Snowboard (Alpine Master) and Far West Masters. Step-by-step instructions: How to join or renew.

New to Far West Masters? Email membership@farwestmasters.org before you register.

1. Renew U.S. Ski & Snowboard by October 15 to avoid the price increase: my.usskiandsnowboard.org. New to Masters? See the Getting Started guide.

2. Renew Far West Masters on Admin Ski Racing, open October 1: masters.adminskiracing.com/farwest. Dues are $20 for the season through November 23, then $25.

3. Register for races in November on the same site. We’ll email you when registration opens.

New this season on Admin Ski Racing

• Review your racer profile. The site has been reset for the new season. The first time you log in, you’ll be asked to review and confirm your racer profile.

• Re-enter your credit card. Saved card info has been cleared and must be entered again.

• New payment processor. ASR has moved to Stripe after last season’s charging problems, and the card entry screens in your profile have been reworked.

Questions? raceadmin@farwestmasters.org (registration)  •  membership@farwestmasters.org (membership)
