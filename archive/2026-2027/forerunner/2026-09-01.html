A Note from Hamish

Hello and welcome to the end of summer update.

You may wonder why I am writing the update rather than Rees. As some of you may know Rees had an unexpected medical setback shortly after the end of last season, but he is making good progress now. As a result of this Rees decided to step down from his role as Division Chair of Far West Masters. We held a special board meeting in July where Rees nominated me as his interim successor as Division Chair and the board voted to confirm my appointment. Please join us in thanking Rees for his many contributions to Far West Masters as Division Chair and in so many other ways over the years. Rees will continue on the board as immediate past Division Chair and Ray Tulloch will step down from that role as Rees succeeds him. A big thanks to Ray for his many contributions also. I would like to thank Rees and the rest of the board for their confidence in me to succeed Rees. According to the bylaws the role of Division Chair will be up for election by a vote of all members in March next year.

I hope everyone is enjoying the summer and looking forward to next season. The board met again in August and started to make plans for next season, more on that to come in September. Mike Braun has put together a good season schedule, with a return after a number of years to Sierra at Tahoe, despite the challenges of working with all of the resorts and race departments to find hill space to put on our races.

Mary Feddis has notified us of her intent to resign as the Vice Chair of Membership of Far West Masters. This is a very important role and we thank Mary very much for the excellent work she has done for us in that role. Mary will continue to assist and help mentor her successor through to the end of 2026. We are actively looking at options for Mary’s successor. If you think you can make a contribution in this area please let me know.

As you all know our snowfall last year was widely regarded as one of the worst on record in the West, despite that we managed to put on some great race days and the season ended with a very enjoyable US Masters National Championships at Mammoth in March. With the prospect of a potentially historic El Niño forecasters are optimistic that we should have a better season for 2026-2027. Opensnow favors above normal snowfall in California and the Southwest U.S. Here’s hoping they are correct and we have a great season ahead.

Hamish
 

2026-27 Preliminary Schedule

Mike Braun has put together next season’s schedule: 17 races currently on the calendar: 6 SL, 5 GS, 6 SG. A few dates may still be added, so treat this as preliminary.

The Bernard Cup will be the Palisades GS weekend, March 6-7. The Viva Italia Cup location hasn’t been finalized. Running it back-to-back with Bernard Cup at Alpine is likely too much racing in one stretch, so we’re looking at either Sierra-at-Tahoe (Feb 6-7) or Sugar Bowl (Feb 27-28) instead. We’ll confirm as soon as it’s locked in.
